About us

Our story

In 1998 the Santos family opened six bamboo cottages on a quiet stretch of the bay. Guests kept coming back, so we kept building, slowly and with local hands.

Today the resort has 48 rooms, but it still runs like a family house: the front desk knows your name and the kitchen knows what you like.

Mission & values

Hospitality

Every guest is treated like a houseguest, not a booking number.

Sustainability

Solar water heating, refillable bottles and a reef nursery we maintain with local divers.

Community

Most of our team and nearly all of our produce come from within 30 km.
